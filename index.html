How to watch this

  1. Five copies of one trained sprinter stand side by side.
  2. Four of them practised for the same time in different ways: with a hip device in half of the runs, with a brake in half, with the device always on, or normally (the twin).
  3. Now they race with the device taken off. Whoever pulls ahead of the twin practised better.
